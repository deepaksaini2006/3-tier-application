How to apply

Four steps from form to enrollment.

  1. Submit the online form

    Fill in the form below with your details and the program you want.

  2. Receive your reference number

    Keep it safe. You will use it in every message to the admission office.

  3. Verify your documents

    Bring originals of your mark sheets, ID proof and entrance score card to the campus.

  4. Pay the fee and confirm your seat

    Your seat is confirmed after document verification and fee payment.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Who can apply for B.Tech?

Students who passed Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics are typically eligible. Exact criteria and entrance requirements are listed by the admission office.

Can I apply for more than one program?

Yes. Submit a separate application for each program.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Ask the admission office about current availability and fees when you receive your reference number.

What documents should I keep ready?

Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, entrance score card if applicable, ID proof, passport-size photographs and a transfer certificate.
